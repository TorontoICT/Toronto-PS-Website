Tools for the Modern Educator

Presented by SEALE J.R

Dashboard & Overview

Your daily command center:

  • At a Glance: View upcoming events and notifications.
  • Quick Actions: Shortcuts to mark attendance or enter grades.
  • Alerts: Stay updated on school announcements.

Class Management

Attendance

  • Digital weekly registers.
  • Export to PDF or Excel.
  • Track absenteeism trends.

Rosters

  • View class lists instantly.
  • Excel Upload: Bulk import learners to build your class list.

Academic Management

Streamline your grading process:

  • Digital Gradebook: Enter marks for assignments and tests.
  • Mark Sheets: Automatically generate printable mark sheets for official records.
  • Calculations: System handles percentages and achievement levels (1-7).

Learner Profiles

Know your students better:

  • Comprehensive View: Access personal info, medical history, and parent contacts.
  • Editing: Update learner details directly from the portal.
  • Documents: View uploaded birth certificates and reports.

Communication Center

Connect with parents effortlessly:

  • Direct Chat: WhatsApp-style messaging interface.
  • Multi-Channel: Send via Portal, Email, or SMS.
  • Templates: Use pre-written messages for feedback or meeting requests.

Chat Interface

AI Content Generator

Powered by Gemini AI

  • Lesson Plans: Generate detailed plans with objectives and activities in seconds.
  • Quizzes & Assessments: Create custom tests based on topic and difficulty.
  • History: Save and review your generated content.

Course Materials

Share resources directly with your learners:

  • Upload Files: Share PDFs, Word documents, or images.
  • Targeted Sharing: Select specific subjects and grades for each upload.
  • Organization: Provide clear descriptions for easy access by students.

Professional Portfolio

Manage your evidence of work:

  • Digital Storage: Upload lesson plans, student work samples, and QMS documents.
  • Organization: Categorized by subject and document type.
  • Sharing: Generate secure links to share your portfolio with SMT or district officials.

Assessment Programme

  • Planning: Schedule assessments for the entire year.
  • Auto-Plan: Use the tool to automatically distribute assessments evenly across the term.
  • Export: Download your programme as PDF or Excel.

Empowering Educators

The Teacher Portal is designed to reduce administrative burden, allowing you to focus on teaching.
