The Homepage

Your starting point for everything.

  • Navigation Bar: Located at the top. Use this to jump to any section (About, Academics, Contact).
  • Hero Section: The main welcome area with quick links to learn more.
  • Footer: Scroll to the bottom for contact info, social media links, and our newsletter subscription.

Everything you need to join our school:

  • Application Form: Access the digital form to apply online.

Accessing Portals

To access your private dashboard:

  1. Click the "Register / Login" button in the top right corner of the menu.
  2. Select your role (Learner, Parent, Teacher, etc.).
  3. Enter your credentials to log in.
Note: Learners use their Admission Number, and Staff use their Special ID.
