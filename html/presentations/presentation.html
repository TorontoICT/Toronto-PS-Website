Toronto Primary School

Digital School Management Ecosystem

Empowering Educators with Next-Gen Technology

Presented by SEALE J.R

The Vision

To transition Toronto Primary School into a fully digital environment that:

  • Reduces administrative paperwork.
  • Enhances communication with parents.
  • Provides real-time academic insights.
  • Uses Artificial Intelligence to assist teachers.

The Digital Ecosystem

A comprehensive platform integrating all aspects of school life:

  • Public Website: Information & News
  • Admissions: Online Applications
  • Learners: Academic Portal

Public Presence & Admissions

Public Website

  • Showcases school identity, academics, and activities.
  • Announcements and events calendar.
  • Contact information and location.

Admissions Portal

  • Digital Applications: Parents apply online.
  • Workflow: Review -> Interview -> Offer -> Acceptance.
  • Database: Auto-populates learner records.

Student & Family Engagement

Learner Portal

  • Access study materials and assignments.
  • View grades and attendance history.
  • Personalized dashboard.

Parent Portal

  • Monitor child's academic progress.
  • Direct communication with teachers.
  • Access financial statements and reports.

The Teacher Portal

A powerful toolkit for modern educators:

  • Class Management: Digital attendance and rosters.
  • Grading: Automated mark sheets and calculations.
  • Portfolio: Digital professional portfolio.
  • Communication: WhatsApp-style chat with parents.

AI Content Generator

Innovation in Lesson Planning

Teachers can instantly generate:

  • Quizzes & Assessments: Tailored to grade and subject.
  • Lesson Plans: Detailed objectives and activities.
Powered by advanced AI to save preparation time.

Leadership & Administration

  • Admin Portal: Centralized control of SA-SAMS data, user accounts, and system configuration.
  • SMT Portal:
    • QMS: Digital Quality Management System for staff appraisals.
    • Analytics: Insights into school performance.
    • Oversight: Monitoring of curriculum coverage.
